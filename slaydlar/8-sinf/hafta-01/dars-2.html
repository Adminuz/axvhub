Hujjat skeleti

Har bir HTML fayl shu to'rt qismdan boshlanadi

Skelet: kim kimning ichida

Qismning ustiga sichqoncha olib boring yoki bosing.

Eng kichik to'liq sahifa


<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="uz">
  <head>
    <title>Mening sahifam</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Salom!</h1>
  </body>
</html>

Eng kichik to'liq sahifa


<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="uz">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <title>Mening sahifam</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Salom!</h1>
  </body>
</html>
Yangi qator: meta charset. U o'zbekcha harflarni to'g'ri ko'rsatadi.
