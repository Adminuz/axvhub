Guruhlash mexanizmi

Shaharlar bo'yicha sotuv


SELECT Shahar,
       COUNT(*)   AS SotuvSoni,
       SUM(Summa) AS Jami
FROM Sotuvlar
GROUP BY Shahar;
-- Toshkent  | 4 | 5380000
-- Samarqand | 3 | 9640000
-- Buxoro    | 3 | 3360000
Natijada 10 emas, 3 qator chiqadi — shaharlar soni qancha bo'lsa, shuncha qator. Qatorlar tartibi har xil bo'lishi mumkin.

Mini-test

GROUP BY Shahar natijasida nechta qator chiqadi (3 ta shahar bor)?

-- XATO: Summa guruhlanmagan va funksiyada emas
SELECT Shahar, Summa
FROM Sotuvlar
GROUP BY Shahar;

-- TO'G'RI
SELECT Shahar, SUM(Summa) AS Jami
FROM Sotuvlar
GROUP BY Shahar;
Xato xabaridagi ustun nomiga qarang: aynan o'sha ustunni aggregatsiyaga o'rang yoki GROUP BY ga qo'shing.

Mini-test

SELECT Shahar, Summa ... GROUP BY Shahar nega xato?

Har bir ustun funksiya ichida yoki GROUP BY da bo'lishi shart.

SELECT Shahar, Kategoriya, SUM(Summa) AS Jami
FROM Sotuvlar
GROUP BY Shahar, Kategoriya
ORDER BY Shahar, Kategoriya;
-- 8 qator: Buxoro/Aksessuar 160000, Buxoro/Kompyuter 3200000, ...

SELECT Shahar, SUM(Summa) AS Jami
FROM Sotuvlar
WHERE Kategoriya = N'Aksessuar'
GROUP BY Shahar
ORDER BY Jami DESC;
-- Toshkent 280000 | Samarqand 240000 | Buxoro 160000
ORDER BY Jami DESC — eng katta yig'indidan boshlab: «reyting» hosil qilishning eng oddiy usuli.
