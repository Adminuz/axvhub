Savol 4/10

Savol 5/10

Savol 6/10

Savol 7/10

Savol 8/10

Savol 9/10

Sikl o'zgaruvchisini o'zgartirib bo'lmaydi.

Savol 10/10

for (a in 1..3) { for (b in 1..2) println(a * b) } nechta qator chiqaradi?

Tashqi 3 marta, ichki har safar 2 marta: 3 * 2 = 6.
