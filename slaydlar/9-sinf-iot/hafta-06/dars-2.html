MicroPython: LED miltillashi


from machine import Pin
import time

led = Pin(2, Pin.OUT)   # ichki LED pini modelga bog'liq

while True:
    led.value(1)
    time.sleep(0.5)
    led.value(0)
    time.sleep(0.5)
MicroPython'da Pin, time kabi modullar ESP ichida ishlaydi. Pin raqami (masalan, 2) plata modeliga bog'liq — hujjatga qarang.

Qo'llanma namunalari

Termometr loyihasi

  1. ESP ga DHT11 ni ulang: VCC — 3,3 V, GND — GND, DATA — raqamli pin.
  2. Dasturda har 5 soniyada haroratni o'qing va Wi-Fi'ga ulanishni tekshiring.
  3. Qiymatni JSON ko'rinishida tuzing va bulutga yuborish yo'lini (HTTP yoki MQTT) tanlang.
