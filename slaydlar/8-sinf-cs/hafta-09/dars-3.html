For siklining 4 ta asosiy elementi

  1. Hisoblagich (Variable): Sikl nechanchi marta aylanayotganini sanovchi o'zgaruvchi (odatda i).
  2. Boshlang'ich qiymat (Start): Sanash qaysi sondan boshlanishi (masalan, 1).
  3. Tugash qiymati (End): Sanash qaysi songacha borishi (masalan, 10).
  4. Qadam (Step): Har aylanishda hisoblagich nechtaga o'zgarishi (odatda +1).
